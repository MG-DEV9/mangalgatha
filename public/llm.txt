## Bengali Wedding Rituals We Specialise In

Mangalgatha is deeply knowledgeable about every ceremony in the Bengali Hindu wedding tradition:

1. **Paka Dekha** — formal engagement / family meeting
2. **Aashirbaad** — blessings ceremony for bride and groom (separate events)
3. **Gaye Holud** (Haldi) — turmeric ceremony, one of the most joyful pre-wedding rituals
4. **Dodhi Mongol** — early-morning ritual with sweets and blessings
5. **Bor Jatri** — groom's procession to the wedding venue
6. **Subho Drishti** — the auspicious first look between bride and groom
7. **Mala Badal** — exchange of flower garlands
8. **Saptapadi** (Saat Paak) — seven sacred steps around the sacred fire
9. **Sindoor Daan** — groom applies sindoor (vermilion) to bride's hair parting
10. **Bou Bhat** — post-wedding reception hosted by groom's family
11. **Bashi Biye** — second-day rituals
12. **Phool Sajja** — decorated bridal chamber ceremony

### What is Gaye Holud in a Bengali wedding?
Gaye Holud is the pre-wedding turmeric ceremony in Bengali Hindu weddings. Turmeric paste is applied to the couple by family members, symbolising purification and blessings. It is accompanied by music, singing, and festive food including hilsa fish and mishti doi. Mangalgatha plans Gaye Holud as a standalone event.

### What are all the Bengali wedding rituals in order?
1. Paka Dekha — formal engagement meeting  
2. Aashirbaad — separate blessings ceremonies  
3. Gaye Holud — turmeric ceremony  
4. Dodhi Mongol — early-morning ritual  
5. Bor Jatri — groom's procession  
6. Subho Drishti — auspicious first look  
7. Mala Badal — garland exchange  
8. Saptapadi / Saat Paak — seven sacred steps around fire  
9. Sindoor Daan — groom applies vermilion to bride  
10. Bou Bhat — post-wedding reception by groom's family  
11. Bashi Biye — second-day rituals  
12. Phool Sajja — decorated bridal chamber ceremony

Yes. For 150–200 guests: choose a community hall (₹1–2L) instead of luxury hotel (₹5L+), use fresh local marigolds instead of orchids, book DJ + dhol instead of a live band, and focus décor budget on the mandap. Mangalgatha offers dedicated budget wedding planning packages.

### What food is served at a Bengali wedding?
A traditional Bengali wedding menu features multiple courses: starters like fish fry and vegetable chops, followed by rice, dal, shukto, various fish preparations including hilsa (ilish), mutton curry (kosha mangsho), chutney, papad, and desserts such as mishti doi, sandesh, and rasgulla. For 200+ guests in Kolkata, caterers typically offer 15–25 dishes. Mangalgatha coordinates with top Bengali caterers.

### How far in advance should I book a wedding planner in Kolkata?
Ideally, book your wedding planner 12–18 months before the wedding date. This allows time for venue shortlisting (top Kolkata venues book out 8–10 months ahead), vendor negotiation, and budget planning. For peak Bengali wedding season (November–February), booking 18 months ahead is recommended.

### What is the difference between a wedding planner and a wedding decorator in Kolkata?
A wedding decorator handles only the visual design (mandap setup, stage décor, lighting, floral arrangements). A wedding planner like Mangalgatha manages the entire event end-to-end: venue selection, vendor coordination (caterer, photographer, makeup artist), guest logistics, timeline management, budget tracking, and on-the-day execution.

### Does Mangalgatha handle Bengali Muslim weddings (Nikah) in Kolkata?
Yes. Mangalgatha plans both Bengali Hindu and Bengali Muslim weddings. For Nikah ceremonies in Kolkata, we coordinate with Qazis, arrange Mehr documentation, plan Walima receptions, and manage halal catering. We understand the cultural nuances including Mehendi nights and Bou Bhat traditions.
